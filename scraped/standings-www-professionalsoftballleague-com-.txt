WHAT MAKES THE PSL SPECIAL?
LOCAL TEAMS.
NATIONAL STRENGTH.
DEEP COMMUNITY
ROOTS.

PSL teams are independently owned and rooted in the communities they serve, supported by the structure and resources of a national professional league.

Local team owners have a stake in building teams that last and become part of the fabric of their communities.

Community is central to the PSL. The goal is to build teams that matter in their markets, create opportunities around the game, give fans and young athletes access to local heroes, and strengthen the connection between professional softball and the people and places that support it.
